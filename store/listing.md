## Description (max 4000)
```
OTF Workouts shows you the day's Orangetheory-style class before you walk in — laid out in clean, scannable Tread, Rower, and Floor blocks instead of buried in a long comment thread.

The workouts are sourced from the public r/orangetheory community, where members post the day's template every morning. OTF Workouts reads that thread, formats it, credits the member who posted it, and links back to the original.

WHAT YOU GET
• Today's class, formatted into easy-to-read blocks
• The last week of workouts, so you can catch up or plan ahead
• Optional notifications — get a push the moment the day's workout is posted, held until 6am your local time so you're never woken up
• Other members' variations for different studios and formats

FAST, PRIVATE, NO ACCOUNT
• No sign-up, no login, no ads, no tracking
• Notifications are opt-in; if you skip them, the app sends us nothing

IMPORTANT
OTF Workouts is an independent, unofficial app. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to Orangetheory Fitness or Reddit. Workouts are written by community members and can vary between studios; always follow your coach. "Orangetheory" and "OTF" are trademarks of their respective owner.
```
