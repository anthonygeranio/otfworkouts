Daily Workouts for OTF — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 25, 2026

Daily Workouts for OTF ("the app") is an independent, unofficial app that shows the daily workout that members of the r/orangetheory community post publicly on Reddit. This policy explains what the app collects and why. The short version: the app has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, and no tracking, and it collects the minimum needed to send you a notification if you choose to turn one on.

What we collect

If — and only if — you turn on notifications, the app sends the following to our notification server so we can deliver the alert:

  • A push notification token. This is an anonymous identifier issued by Apple/Google (via Expo) that lets us send a notification to your device. It is not your name, email, phone number, or Apple/Google account.
  • Your device's time zone (e.g. America/New_York). We use this only to hold notifications until 6 a.m. your local time so you are not alerted overnight.

If you never turn on notifications, the app sends us none of this.

What we do NOT collect

  • No account, name, email address, or password.
  • No precise or approximate location (a time-zone name is not your location).
  • No advertising identifiers, and we do not use any third-party analytics or ad networks.
  • No contacts, photos, health data, or device sensors.

How your information is used

The push token and time zone are used solely to send you one notification per day when that day's workout is posted, at an appropriate local hour. We do not sell, rent, or share this data, and we do not use it for advertising or to build a profile of you.

Where the workout content comes from

Workouts are written by members of the public r/orangetheory subreddit and read through Reddit's public RSS feeds. The app shows each contributor's Reddit username and links back to the original post. The app is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to Reddit, Inc. or Orangetheory Fitness. "Orangetheory" and "OTF" are trademarks of their respective owner and are used here only to describe the community whose posts the app displays.

Data storage and retention

Push tokens and time zones are stored on our server (Cloudflare) only while notifications are enabled. When you turn notifications off, or uninstall the app, the token is removed. Workout content is cached briefly (a few hours) and refreshed, so content deleted on Reddit drops out.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.
